💡 Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I write poems in other languages?
Yes! Versilume seamlessly supports 127 languages. Gemini translates poetic meaning and cultural nuance while preserving rhythm before agent extraction.
How do the multi-agents work?
Three dedicated Llama-3.2 agents run in parallel: Emotion Agent captures tone, Semantic Agent identifies themes, and Metaphor Agent converts abstract verse into concrete visual prompts.
How does multi-scene detection work?
Long poems are automatically segmented into distinct visual scenes based on emotional and narrative shifts, generating a cohesive storyboard of artworks.
Can I download full-resolution artworks?
Yes, simply hover over any rendered artwork to click the download button, or click the frame to open high-resolution lightbox inspection.
